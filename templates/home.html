🧠 SYSTEM INTELLIGENCE CORE

The Neural Threat Matrix operates as an advanced cybersecurity intelligence system engineered to analyze, interpret, and respond to system-level events through continuous log monitoring.

At its core, the system ingests raw log streams and converts them into structured data, enabling deep behavioral analysis of system activity. Every log entry becomes a signal that contributes to a larger intelligence model.

The platform applies rule-based anomaly detection techniques to identify irregular patterns such as elevated error rates, abnormal warning distributions, and unusual operational spikes.

A dynamic risk computation engine evaluates these anomalies and assigns a quantified risk score, classifying system health into multiple severity levels ranging from stable to critical.

Beyond detection, the system generates contextual explanations, transforming technical log data into human-readable insights that assist in rapid decision-making and threat response.

This architecture simulates a lightweight Security Operations Center (SOC), integrating monitoring, detection, visualization, and reporting into a unified intelligent interface.

Designed for scalability, the system can be extended to real-time streaming, machine learning-based anomaly detection, and cloud-native deployment environments.
